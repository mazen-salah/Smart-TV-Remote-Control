# Privacy Policy

**Smart TV Remote** (`com.summationworks.smarttvremote`) does not collect,
store, or transmit any personal data.

## What the app does with the network

- It searches your local Wi-Fi network for Samsung and LG TVs using standard
  discovery protocols (SSDP and Bonjour/mDNS) and talks to the TV you pick
  over that same local network.
- It never contacts a server of ours. There is no account, no analytics, no
  advertising, and no crash reporting.

## What is stored on your phone

- The TVs you have paired with (name, IP address, MAC address, model) so they
  can be shown and reconnected quickly.
- The pairing token or client key the TV issued, so the TV does not ask you
  to allow the app again on every launch.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on the device and is removed
when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions

- **Local network / nearby devices**: required to find and control TVs.
- **Vibration**: haptic feedback on button presses.
